# Asks — Mars RAD learning cycles

Each open gap below is a place a contribution can land. A contribution is one cycle in this repository's ledger, under your name. How to contribute, and the templates: https://github.com/Nervous-Machine/DEBRIEF/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md

## 1. The two bumps in dust season, February and May to June 2024 (ledger id G3)

The timing of the bumps has an account. Their size at Mars is unmeasured, and whether dust plays a part could not be tested.

Status: immovable.

- **dust-opacity maps for Mars Year 37, or the rover optical-depth record extended past sol 3953** — contribution kind: data; who could act: someone who can reach the record keepers.
- **solar wind measured at Mars (ESCAPADE, about 2028)** — contribution kind: data; who could act: the ESCAPADE team; an outreach note is drafted and not sent.
- **a research-grade filter for MAVEN's upstream solar wind** — contribution kind: claim or research; who could act: a heliophysics collaborator.

Signal expected: not yet registered. Resolution by: none registered.
